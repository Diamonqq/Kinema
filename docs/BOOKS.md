# Choose your book

Open Chapters or Library → Problems. The book chooser keeps each library's
search, filters, question identities, help and conversations separate.

## Kinema practice

Included without an upload or sign-in: 635 original questions, made from 112
setups with parameter variants across 14 chapters. Hints, formulas and worked
answers are prepared locally. Questions use the familiar chapter/number grid
as reference labels; they are different from Morin's corresponding questions
and do not reproduce the textbook's full topic coverage or difficulty.

These questions have no textbook scans or matched problem simulations. Library's
general simulations remain available. More genuinely different setups can be
added without changing a textbook question's identity.

## Your Morin textbook

1. Choose **Morin · your textbook**.
2. Click **Add textbook PDF** and select your existing PDF.
3. After verification, all 635 checked questions and exercises open immediately.

The prepared import supports the PDF with SHA-256:

```text
51fbce11159680fe2a529c1866e93f584278cf5d52b4cd6abcf9869c115ac991
```

Its size is 4,233,289 bytes. A filename does not establish compatibility. A
different edition, scan or re-saved PDF needs its own prepared recipe; the app
rejects it and keeps the current library intact. It does not silently fall back
to approximate OCR or make AI calls.

The app reads text from your PDF and applies checked formatting instructions.
Those instructions use PDF positions and math markup. The public release has
no source PDF, copied page images or complete textbook question prose. The
imported source and reconstructed catalog stay under the app's data directory,
inside `books/morin`. An import is activated only after the complete pack checks
successfully. Repeating the same import reuses it and preserves your saved work.

The local pack includes checked native statements, original numbering,
question/figure/solution crop locations, prepared help where it exists, and
the existing matched simulations. A crop is rendered from your PDF when needed.
All questions have tutor entry points; not all have prepared solutions or
simulations. Tutor-generated help requires your own connected account.

Daily uses the imported selection and requires a source-matched prepared visual.
The ten-minute clock still starts only when you press Start. Importing a book
does not copy someone's chats, study history, photos or provider credentials.

## Other books

This version has two book choices. It does not yet prepare arbitrary textbooks.
A new book needs an edition-specific import recipe, its own reference map and
checked question/help bindings. Unsupported PDFs can still be attached to a
normal tutor chat; that does not turn them into a prepared whole-book library.
